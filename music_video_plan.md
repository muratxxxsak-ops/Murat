## Shot list (cut every 2-4 beats; hard cuts on downbeats)
1. **0:00 Intro.** Dawn coast road. Parked sport bike, mist, a gloved hand on the tank. Slow push-in, 85 mm, shallow depth of field.
2. **0:08 Intro.** Macro: key turn, dash lights up, exhaust heat shimmer.
3. **0:16 Verse 1.** Rider pulls out, low tracking shot, city at blue hour. Cut on every downbeat.
4. **0:24-0:56 Verse 1.** Alternate: helmet-visor reflections, handlebar POV, drone chase, lane splitting between neon-lit traffic.
5. **0:56 Build.** Rider enters a tunnel. Sodium lights strobe past in time with the kick. Speed ramp (slow to fast) into the chorus.
6. **1:04 Chorus.** First wheelie on the lyric hit. Low rear-follow camera, sparks and light streaks.
7. **1:20 Breather.** Wide: bike parked on a cliff above the sea, golden hour. Rider sits, looks out.
8. **1:36-2:00 Chorus 2.** Rapid cuts: sharp lean into corners (knee near the ground), downshift close-ups, rain-slick streets, reflections.
9. **2:00 Drop.** Freeze or slow-motion on the bike, then a full silence-style beat.
10. **2:08-2:40 Final chorus.** Longest wheelie, tunnel sprint, burnout, fireworks of sparks. Shorten shots to 1 beat near the end.
11. **2:40 Outro.** Sunset beach. Rider removes helmet, walks away, bike in silhouette. Fade out.

## Look
- ARRI-style color: teal and orange shadows and highlights, soft halation on lights, fine film grain.
- Anamorphic flares on tunnel lights, 2.39:1.
- Fully geared rider: matte black race suit, armored jacket, full-face helmet with reflective visor. Do not show the face.

## Assembly
1. Generate or shoot clips per the shot list.
2. Edit in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, or CapCut. Place markers at each downbeat (about every 0.93 s) and cut on them.
3. Add speed ramps at the tunnel entries, a film-grain overlay, and a teal-orange grade.
4. Add engine and tire sound effects at low volume under the music for realism.

## Realism note
Today's AI video models can handle close-ups, tunnel and city ride shots, and parked scenic shots well. Long wheelies, exact rider anatomy, and perfect bike physics are the usual weak spots. For those, short clips and a real stunt rider or stock footage are safer.
